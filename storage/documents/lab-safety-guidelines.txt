=========================================
CAMPUSHUB - LABORATORY SAFETY GUIDELINES
=========================================
1. Workstation Hygiene:
   - Always log out of shared portal accounts before leaving the laboratory.
   - Do not plug unauthorized USB drives into laboratory workstations.

2. Network & Testing Policy:
   - Testing security tools is strictly restricted to designated isolated targets.
   - Respect peer privacy and adhere to the institutional code of conduct.

3. Assignment Submissions:
   - Ensure all coursework uploads meet designated file formats (.pdf, .txt, .docx).
   - Report any portal irregularities to admin@campushub.local immediately.
